🔬 Academic Measurement Context: CodeCarbon vs GMT

Important Distinction: CodeCarbon estimates energy consumption (kWh) and carbon emissions (kg CO2eq) using CPU model TDP (Thermal Design Power), process CPU utilization, and regional electricity emission intensity factors. In contrast, the Green Metrics Tool (GMT) uses hardware-level telemetry (Intel/AMD RAPL energy registers) and Linux kernel cgroups within an end-to-end containerized scenario.
